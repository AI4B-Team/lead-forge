Billing Webhooks

Realeflow keeps your users' platform accounts in sync with their billing state automatically. You point your billing platform's webhooks at our webhook service and tag your products with a small amount of metadata — from then on, accounts provision, upgrade, suspend, and deactivate themselves in lockstep with billing. No manual account management and no custom integration code on your side.

Your billing platform ──▶ Realeflow webhook service ──▶ your white-label platform

What stays in sync​

Regardless of billing platform, the same lifecycle is handled:

Billing event	What happens on your platform
New subscription	The user's account is created on the plan mapped to what they bought.
Plan change (upgrade / downgrade)	The account's plan changes to match.
Payment failure	The account is suspended.
Payment recovered	The account is reactivated.
Cancellation	The account is deactivated. This is permanent — deactivation removes the user's data.
One-time addon purchase	The addon (feature access or credits) is granted to the user's account.

Only products tagged with our metadata are acted on. Anything untagged is silently ignored, so you can safely route your entire billing account at our endpoint — unrelated products and subscriptions are skipped.

What you provide​

The specifics vary by platform (see the platform guides below), but onboarding always comes down to three things:

Webhook endpoints — point your billing platform's webhooks at our service, for both your live and test environments.
Product mapping — metadata on your prices/products that tells us which Realeflow plan or addon each one corresponds to. We supply the plan IDs and addon codes for your product lineup during onboarding.
Credentials — a webhook signing secret (so we can verify events really came from your account) and a minimal read-only API key (so we can look up customer details like email and name when creating accounts).

Alternatively, most platforms let you grant Realeflow limited access to your billing account (e.g. a developer-role team member), in which case we handle all of the above for you — usually the fastest path. Each platform guide covers both options.
